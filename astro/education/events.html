How it works

From club to Grand Finale

  1. 01Register

    Your college club creates an account and registers a team for each challenge.

  2. 02Zonal event

    Compete at a zonal event. Hands-on workshops run during the zonal events too.

  3. 03Grand Finale

    The top 20 teams from each zone in each challenge go to the Grand Finale.
