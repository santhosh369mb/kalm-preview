TheBoxingBots

Where robotics becomes a sport

BoxingBot is a do-it-yourself humanoid fighting robot kit made by KAL-M. Students build it, code it and battle it. It took more than 50 design iterations to get a bot that is strong, stable and affordable.

  1. 01Build

    Assemble the bot from the kit: legs, hips, body, punching arms and wheels.

  2. 02Code

    Program it on an ESP32 board, from the first motor test to special moves.

  3. 03Battle

    Fight it in the arena, see what breaks, improve it, fight again.

What students learn

It comes with a course

  1. Stage 1

    Build

    Motors and servos, chassis, body, the ESP32, motor driver, the BoxingBot circuit board, buck converter, pins, Arduino IDE.

  2. Stage 2

    Code

    Program structure, variables, control structures, libraries and COM ports, PS4 and wireless controller set-up.

  3. Stage 3

    Control

    Motor and servo control, button mapping, punch control, hip control, full combo code, autonomous mode, endurance test.

  4. Stage 4

    Battle

    Special moves (block, attack, jam combo, finisher), move-exchange challenge, showcase, battle strategy and scoring, championship preparation.

FAQ

Common questions

Who is it for?

Ages 8 and above: school and college students, makers and robotics clubs.

Do I need to know coding?

No. The course starts with motors and servos, then teaches Arduino coding on the ESP32 step by step.

How is the bot controlled?

With the Bluetooth game controller in the kit. Students program it in the Arduino IDE (C++).

How long does the battery take to charge?

About 2.4 hours.
